Privacy Policy — Tama Booth

Effective date: November 22, 2026
Developer: Othelo Beating
Contact: beating.othelo@gmail.com

Tama Booth is a photo booth app that runs on your own Android device. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your photos stay on your device unless you choose to upload them, and the app collects no personal data about you, shows no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking.

1. Photos and videos

Photos, GIFs, boomerangs and videos you take with Tama Booth are saved locally on your device, in your Pictures folder under TamaBooth / <event name>. They are not sent anywhere by the app on its own. Only the people at your event who use the booth can see them on the booth device, and only the event host can delete them.

2. Optional cloud upload (your choice)

If the event host turns on cloud upload in Settings and signs in, finished photos may be uploaded to the host's own Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive account, in a TamaBooth / <event name> folder. This happens only when the host enables it and signs in with their own account. The app only ever accesses files it creates itself in that folder (Google Drive drive.file scope; OneDrive file access for the app's own folder). Sign-in tokens are stored on the device only. Guests who scan a download QR receive a link to that one photo only. If cloud upload is off, nothing is uploaded.

3. What the app does not collect

Tama Booth does not create accounts, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and does not collect or send analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or location data. The app contains no ads and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. Permissions the app uses, and why

  • Camera — to take booth photos and videos, and to scan QR codes for pairing.
  • Internet and Wi-Fi state — to run the booth's local web server on your own Wi-Fi network (so a guest phone can act as a remote camera or view the event gallery), and to upload to Google Drive / OneDrive only when you enable it.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) — to save photos to your Pictures folder.
  • Notifications and foreground service — to keep the booth server running while an event is live.
  • Wake lock — to keep the screen on during a booth session.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, or SMS permissions.

5. Local network (web booth and event gallery)

The booth can serve a web page on your local Wi-Fi network so guest phones can take photos or browse the active event's gallery. This is served directly from your device over your own network — photos are not sent to any Tama Booth server, because there is none. Access to the web booth, gallery and settings is protected by the 4-digit PIN the host sets in the app.

6. Printing

If the host enables printing, photos are sent directly from the device to a printer on the same Wi-Fi network (IPP) or connected by USB. Photos are not routed through any third-party print service.

7. Children

Tama Booth is a general-audience event app operated by an adult host. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores lives on your device. You can delete events and photos from inside the app's gallery at any time. Uninstalling the app removes the app's stored settings; photos in your Pictures folder remain until you delete them yourself. If you enabled cloud upload, files already uploaded to your Google Drive or OneDrive stay in your account until you delete them there.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. The app itself will not notify you of policy changes, so please check back here if you need the current version.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or about Tama Booth can be sent to the contact email listed at the top of this page.
